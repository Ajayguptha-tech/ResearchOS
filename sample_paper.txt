# Graph Neural Networks for Drug Discovery: A Comprehensive Survey

## Abstract
Recent advances in geometric deep learning and graph neural networks (GNNs) have transformed computational drug discovery. This paper systematically categorizes molecular graph representations, message-passing architectures, and benchmark datasets including MoleculeNet and ChEMBL. We analyze key methodological challenges including molecular conformal invariance, over-squashing in deep GNNs, and out-of-distribution generalization on clinical target profiles.

## Methodology
We evaluate equivariant message passing neural networks (EGNNs) and directional graph transformers against molecular property prediction tasks. The model employs multi-relational graph convolutions with distance-dependent edge features.

## Results
On the MoleculeNet benchmark (BACE and BBBP datasets), the proposed equivariant architecture achieves an ROC-AUC of 0.924 (+0.041 improvement over baseline GCN). Virtual screening validation on 10,000 candidate ligands identified 3 novel active compounds confirmed by in-vitro assays.

## Discussion and Future Work
Key research gaps include 3D binding pocket representation, multi-target selectivity prediction, and uncertainty quantification for Bayesian drug optimization.
